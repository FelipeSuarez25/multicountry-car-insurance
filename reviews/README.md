`US-NN-review-RN.md`. The format, severities and checklist are in the `review-checklist` skill.

`Resolves: US-NN-RN-FNN`, and the fix is verified in the next round. Human comments made on the
PR at HC3 are turned into findings in the next round, so all corrections stay in one place.
